\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec-conclude}

We have presented \hermes, which treats natural language to SPARQL as
verified query planning over a typed SPARQL algebra.
Plans are typed against the RDF schema and checked without execution;
actions are algebra operators whose effect on the graph is observed;
and a plan is accepted only when it is valid and satisfies a goal
derived from the question.
On the Text2SPARQL 2026 corporate benchmark with Qwen3 models from 4B
to 14B parameters, \hermes is the most accurate method at 14B
($F_1$ 0.282), and its accuracy grows fastest with model size.
The verifier cuts the items without a runnable query at every size, but
raises accuracy only at 14B: most of the queries it recovers from the
smaller models score zero.
No method answers ASK questions, whose answer shape a goal can state in
advance.
Next steps are API and larger open models, a budget-matched comparison,
ablations of the observation, the DBpedia and Wikidata benchmarks, and
the proofs in Appendix~A.
