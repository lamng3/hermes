\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec-conclude}

We have presented \hermes, which treats natural language to SPARQL as
verified query planning over a typed SPARQL algebra.
Plans are typed against the RDF schema and checked without execution;
actions are algebra operators whose effect on the graph is observed;
and a plan is accepted only when it is valid and satisfies a goal
derived from the question.
On the Text2SPARQL 2026 corporate benchmark with an 8B open model,
operator-level planning outperforms one-shot generation and
constraint-guided search and almost always ends with a runnable query,
at a higher call budget.
All methods fail on aggregation and ASK questions, whose answer shape a
goal can state in advance.
Next steps are the evaluation of the full verified planner, a
budget-matched comparison, ablations of the observation, the DBpedia
and Wikidata benchmarks, and the proofs in Appendix~A.
